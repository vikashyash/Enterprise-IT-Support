# Password and passphrase policy

This policy covers AetherTech network passwords, email, VPN, and SSO. It does not cover personal accounts.

## Rules

- Minimum 14 characters. A passphrase of four or more words is preferred.
- Do not reuse a password from the last 12 changes.
- Passwords expire every 180 days. MFA does not remove this requirement.
- Do not share a password in chat, email, or a ticket comment.
- Do not store passwords in a shared spreadsheet. Use the company password manager.

## Self-service reset

1. Go to https://aka.ms/sspr or the sign-in portal **Forgot password** link.
2. Verify with Authenticator, not SMS, when both are registered.
3. Set a new passphrase that you have not used here before.

If self-service reset is locked after too many attempts, call the service desk. IT will not reset a password from an unverified chat message.

## What IT will never do in chat

The support agent will not ask you to type your current password. It will not email you a temporary password. A message that asks for your password is phishing — report it.

## After a suspected leak

If you pasted a work password into a public site, change it immediately through self-service and then report a security concern. Do not wait for IT to notice.
