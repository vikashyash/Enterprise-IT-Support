# New employee IT onboarding

This checklist is for an employee's first week at AetherTech.

## Day 0 (before you arrive)

HR creates your account. You receive a welcome mail at your personal address with a one-time setup link. Complete MFA enrolment on that link before day one. The laptop will not finish first login without MFA.

## Day 1

1. Collect your laptop and badge from Reception. Do not take an unassigned spare laptop from a cupboard.
2. Sign in with your AetherTech email. First password change is forced.
3. Connect to **AetherTech-Corp** Wi-Fi. The guest network will not enrol the device.
4. Wait for Company Portal / Self Service to finish. This can take 45 minutes. Do not install random apps during enrolment.
5. Set up the corporate VPN using the Windows or macOS VPN setup article.
6. Open Outlook and Teams. If either asks for a personal Microsoft account, cancel and use your work email.

## Days 2–5

- Request any extra system access you need. Default access is only email, SSO, intranet, HR, and your department drive.
- Confirm payroll and timesheet links on the intranet.
- If you need licensed software on day one (for example Docker Desktop), ask your manager to approve the request the same day. IT will not skip approval for onboarding.

## Starters who join remotely

Ship-to-home laptops arrive enrolled. Connect to home Wi-Fi, complete Company Portal, then VPN. Do not factory-reset a shipped laptop. If it boots to a setup screen you do not recognise, call the service desk before clicking through.
