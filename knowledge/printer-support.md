# Printer and follow-me print

AetherTech uses follow-me printing. You do not install a printer by IP address.

## How to print

1. Install **AetherTech Follow-Me Print** from Company Portal or Self Service.
2. Print to the queue named **Follow-Me-BW** or **Follow-Me-Color**.
3. At any floor printer, tap your badge. The job releases there.
4. Jobs older than 24 hours are deleted.

## Badge will not release

- Wait 10 seconds after the first tap. Double-taps cancel the job.
- If the panel says Unknown card, your badge is not linked. Raise a ticket with the badge number printed on the back.
- Color jobs need Follow-Me-Color. A badge tap on a mono-only device will not show those jobs.

## Paper jams and toner

Do not open the fuser. Send a Hardware ticket with the printer asset tag on the front sticker. Floor coordinators may load paper; they must not replace toner drums.

## Home printing

IT does not install home printers on company laptops. Save to PDF and print from another device.
