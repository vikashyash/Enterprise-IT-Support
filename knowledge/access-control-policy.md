# Access control policy

This policy explains how employees get access to systems, shared drives, and applications.

## Default access

On day one you receive email, SSO, intranet, the HR portal, and your department's shared drive. Nothing else is granted by default. Finance, Legal, Production GitHub orgs, and customer data stores are request-only.

## How to request access

1. Tell the support agent the exact resource name, for example **Finance Shared Drive** or **GitHub org aethertech-prod**.
2. Give a business reason and the role you need (`READ` or `READ_WRITE`).
3. The agent opens an access request. Your manager must approve it.
4. IdentityAccess grants the permission only after approval. The agent will not grant access itself in this phase of the service.

## Typical processing time

- Manager approval: one business day
- Grant after approval: four business hours during 09:00–18:00 IST

Urgent production-down access still needs a manager or an on-call IT_ADMIN. There is no emergency bypass in chat.

## What you should not request

- Access for someone else. That person must ask from their own account.
- Standing admin rights on a laptop. Local admin is an IT_ADMIN exception and is time-limited.
- Copies of another team's mailbox.

## Lost access after a transfer

Department transfers drop old shared-drive permissions the next night. Request the new drive after your HR record shows the new department. IT will not keep both Finance and a previous department drive without a documented exception.
